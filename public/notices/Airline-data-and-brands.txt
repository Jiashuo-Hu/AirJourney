These are reference records, not an authoritative current IATA register.
OpenFlights warns that its Active flag is unreliable. Y is not treated as proof
of current operation. Source-listed inactivity and dissolution are labelled;
unknown status remains unknown. Historic aliases/codes, shared/reused codes and
non-flight transport operators can occur. No codes are invented for missing data.

Original SVGs remain in logos/airlines/originals/; no bitmap was wrapped as SVG.
Missing reliable vectors use a diamond placeholder. No claim of globally complete
or universally current brand artwork is made.

v0.4 includes 115 full vector marks, 92 independently separated graphics, and
retained originals. Coverage by region and explicit gaps: data/airline-sources/
v04-coverage.json. 95 marks were added to the prior 20. Spring Airlines remains
a placeholder: the located SVG embeds a bitmap and was rejected. Wordmark-only
and inseparable variants keep the full mark in the library; default list/picker
views retain the complete wordmark when no symbol is supplied. Integral lettering in symbols
(e.g. TAP monogram or Virgin badge) is intentionally retained.

WorldVectorLogo was considered as a reference; its terms were reviewed at
https://worldvectorlogo.com/terms-of-use. No files were copied from that site:
its terms are not treated as permission for modification or transfer.
Official site HTTP availability is only a fetch result, not proof of exhaustive
brand verification. Specific primary confirmations and uncertain cases remain
in the per-brand manifest. Kenya's white official lockup is presented on navy;
the underlying paths and colors are preserved. No general clearance is claimed.
